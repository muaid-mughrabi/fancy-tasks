> [!tip]- How this page works
> - **Today:** what you're doing, what you planned for today or earlier, and anything due within 2 days, each with its next step. ⚠ means it has none yet. Keep the step small enough to start in 5 minutes; if a task feels too big, write only its first chunk.
> - **Capture in a meeting:** Cmd-N (or the new-note button on the phone), type the title, done. It lands in `Inbox` and shows under *To sort*.
> - **Focus blocks:** recurring morning blocks in your work calendar (e.g. three mornings a week, 90 minutes). Before one starts, set `planned` on the task you'll work on.
> - **Dates:** `deadline`, `submission`, `session` and `defense` go to the Work calendar by themselves. `planned` stays in Obsidian.
> - **Done** adds `closed`. **Waiting** adds `follow_up` a week ahead and turns into 🔔 when it's due.
> - **Send to T3** (🤖 button at the top of a ticket): an agent works on the ticket and drafts emails in Fancy Mail, never sends. The **activity** button (pulse icon in the left bar) shows what happened; each ticket keeps an *Agent log* at the end.
> - **References** keep information for later with nothing to do: right-click a ticket › *Turn into reference* (and back with *Turn back into a task*). They stay off Home and the boards.
> - **Waiting**, **Done this week** are folded: click a title to open it.
> - **Snooze** a ticket (right-click › *Snooze…*): it leaves Home until that day and waits in the folded *Snoozed* section.
> - **Up next:** committed tasks (todo) without a date for today, oldest first. **Backlog** (folded): someday ideas, not committed.
> - Empty sections are faded. To change a section, open `Views/Home.base`.
